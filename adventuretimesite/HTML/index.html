The year is about 3000 AD. A thousand years ago, "the mushroom war" wiped out civilization. In the mean time, magic has been allowed to manifest. The Land of Ooo, where most of Adventure Time takes place, is home to the main characters Finn the Human and Jake the Dog. Evidence of the apocalyptic nature of the war is still present with ruined cities and pre-war detritus scattered about. The characters in the world don't even notice the junk, it's just part of the landscape. One of the main plots is Finn's coming-of-age story. He loves, he learns; you know, human stuff. As he and Jake go on adventures, more detail about the world reveals itself. One such detail is there is a gigantic crater the size of Australia missing from the Earth. Several subplots include the evil Lich attempting to wipe out all life in existence, the politics of the various kingdoms in the land of Ooo, and the quest for Finn to know his biological father.
