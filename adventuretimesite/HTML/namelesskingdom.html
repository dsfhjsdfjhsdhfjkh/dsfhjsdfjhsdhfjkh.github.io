Adventure Time: The Secret of the Nameless Kingdom

Adventure Time: The Secret of the Nameless Kingdom is a video game developed by WayForward Technologies with the help of Pendleton Ward. Unlike the previous titles, this game was published by Little Orbit instead of D3 Publisher. The game was first announced on May 8, 2014, and was released on November 18, 2014.


The game's plot takes Finn and Jake to discover the secrets through uncharted territories within the Land of Ooo.
